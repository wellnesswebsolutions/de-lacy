Welcome to De Lacy

A boutique salon & spa in the heart of Beverley.

De Lacy Hair & Beauty is a boutique salon and spa based in the popular market town of Beverley, in the East Riding of Yorkshire. Our highly trained, experienced team offers an extensive range of hair, beauty and massage treatments in warm, relaxed surroundings.

From a fresh cut and hand-painted balayage to a Million Dollar Elemis facial or a complete bridal package, every appointment begins with a genuine consultation — and ends with you feeling your very best.
